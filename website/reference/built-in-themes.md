# Built-in themes

Vela comes with 21 themes: eight inspired by the classic Material Theme, three Vela originals, and ten ports of favorite editor themes. Every one meets Vela's [contrast targets](/customize/creating-themes#checking-contrast) for readable text.

Try any of them live with **Select Theme** (<kbd>⌃⌘T</kbd>), or start your own from one in the [theme editor](/customize/theme-editor). The swatches under each screenshot show the window and sidebar backgrounds, text, accent, link color and the first few nick colors.

## Credits

The ported themes reuse their projects' published color palettes, adjusted where needed for legibility; the changes are listed in [THEME-CREDITS.md](https://github.com/r3volution11/Vela-IRC/blob/main/docs/THEME-CREDITS.md), along with each project's license. The Material-inspired themes are original Vela designs inspired by the classic Material Theme palettes by Mattia Astorino; Vela isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Material Theme or Vira Software. Vela Graphite, Paper and Mist are original designs.
